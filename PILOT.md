# Bottor Assist – Pilot Program

Bottor Assist is currently in a limited pilot phase with educators.

## What’s being tested
- Rubric-based grading accuracy
- Subject auto-detection
- ELA and Math workflows
- Feedback clarity and usefulness

## Not in scope (yet)
- District-wide integrations
- SIS sync
- Student-facing dashboards

## Feedback
Pilot participants are encouraged to share feedback directly with the project owner.
